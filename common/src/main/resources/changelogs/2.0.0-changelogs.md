## The Final Feast Update || v2.0.0 MC-1.20.1 + MC-1.21.1

----------------------
### As you may have guessed by the title, *this will be the final major update to Dungeon's Delight.*

(This isn't a _Terraria_ situation where I'll say this and then spend 10 years making "final" updates - this is it!)

Betwixer and I have reached the conclusion that - culminating with this update - we've added everything that
we wanted to *Dungeon's Delight*. This update adds the final handful of things that we had left on the back burner (**including
separating the mod from Farmer's Delight!**), as well as some other bits and goodies here and there.

That being said, this is no small update at all; *we've rewritten the entire mod from the ground-up to remove reliance on
Farmer's Delight (although you can still play with it and get the same experience!), **AND to make way for a Fabric port for
both 1.20.1 & 1.21.1!***

*Take a look at all the changes below!*

----------------------

### Notes
- With this update, **THE ENTIRETY OF DUNGEON'S DELIGHT HAS BEEN REWRITTEN FROM THE GROUND UP.** This means several things:
  - Dungeon's Delight no longer requires Farmer's Delight (although you should still have a similar experience to previous 
  updates if you have it installed together!)
  - **MAKE BACKUPS OF YOUR WORLDS!!!** We've done the best we can to ensure everything from prior updates works, but there's no
  guarantee that things won't break! **Consider yourself warned.**
  - With the code being based on the latest 1.21.1 version, most reasonable changes from 1.21.1 have been ported to 1.20.1; refer to version 1.3 - 1.5 changelogs to see the majority of changes

### Removals & Breaking Changes
- Removed Brined Flesh & Gritty Flesh, these felt like "bloat" items that existing items like Rotten Tripe could already fulfill

### Rotten Farming
- Added Terror Preta, a new block used for growing Dungeon's Delight's crops
  - Requires water like normal farmland, and additionally needs to be hidden from sunlight
  - Exposing it to sunlight will cause it to burn like the undead
  - Rotten crops are allowed in any light level unlike vanilla crops

### Misc Additions
- Added Darting, a new uncommon non treasure enchantment for the cleaver
  - Has a max level of III
  - Decreases charge time by 12% per level
  - Increased throw range (I - +0.16, II - +0.33, III - +0.5)
- Added Creeperilla while in its raw form its highly volatile and will explode on impact
  - Consuming Creeperilla with active effects will cause them to leak out in lingering clouds and cause a small explosion dealing some damage

### Cleaver Overhaul
- All cleaver drops have been rebalanced (cleaver drops are also now known as "reapings")
- Thrown cleavers will now always drop reapings (no longer required to hold a cleaver the entire time before the entity dies)
- Entities will no longer drop reapings when the cleaver is not in the attacking hand
- As usual, cleavers have additionally received a few small balance tweaks, below are the changes:
  - Fire Aspect is now allowed on cleavers, reaped drops will be cooked (beware of the fact cooked ingredients cannot be used for cooking)
  - Serrated Strike now goes up to level 3 and is incompatible with Fire Aspect
    - Serrated debuff damage decreased from 2 to 1
    - Serrated Strike levels decrease the piercing damage penalty by 5% per level
    - Serrated Strike levels increase the damage rate of the Serrated debuff by increasing its amplifier (I - 30, II - 15, III - 10)
    - Serrated effect tick rate has been increased from 20 to 30 (nerf)
    - Serrated effect no longer does more damage with each amplifier (was never obtainable in previous versions)
    - Serrated damage now drops cleaver reapings
    - Serrated Strike is now an uncommon enchantment, previously was common rarity
  - Cleavers now require a quarter of their charge to be completed before they can be thrown (8 ticks)
  - Fully charged cleavers now have halved cooldown
  - All cleavers have had their attack damage decreased by -0.5
  - All cleavers have had their attack speed increased by +0.1 (are now faster)
  - All cleavers have had their throwing range decreased by -0.5
  - Flint Cleavers have had their ranged increased by +0.16
  - Golden Cleavers have had their range increased by +0.5

### Wormouth Overhaul
Prior to this update, wormouths didn't really do a lot; they acted as a means of obtaining specific monster ingredients,
but said ingredients were already relatively easy to obtain, giving less incentive to make note of any wormouths you'd come
across. With this update, wormouths have received a fresh coat of paint:
- Wormouth drops are now entirely data-driven! This means that addons or datapackers can add their own wormouth drops:
  - Information is stored in `data/<namespace>/dungeonsdelight/wormouth`
  - Each `.json` file requires a few things:
    - Either a defined `item` or `tag` (**only one can be filled, otherwise it won't load**)
    - The loot table (`table`) to pull from when giving items
    - A boolean for whether the recipe should cause the Wormouth uses to deplete (`exhaust`)
- Wormouths can now provide different items based on what they're fed. Some examples include:
  - What happens if you feed them the Malady Music Disc? Well, why don't you go find out for yourself...
- Feeding Wormouths any items with food value still works the same, with a few changes:
  - The generic drop loot table has been reworked to include mob drops that are hard to obtain in Peaceful difficulty!
- Wormouths can have items directly inserted into them using hoppers, allowing for some (slow) automated feeding!
- When hit with a thrown Cleaver, Wormouths will panic and drop a bunch of random items
  - This will, in turn, make them shut for double the time, making this far more of a gamble than just feeding them normally!
- Added unique sounds for Wormouths & updated consumption animations
  - (Luckily, if for some odd reason you decide you want to break the Wormouth mid-animation, it'll still drop the item. Probably don't do that still)
- Now drop a small bit of EXP most of the time when feeding it
- Can now be waterlogged and rotated for the sake of Creative Mode building
- Now considered a block entity

### Changes
- Many foods in Dungeon's Delight have been rebalanced, below is the full list:
  - Rotten Tripe saturation increased from 0.05 to 0.1 and now consumes fast
  - Spider Meat nutrition increased from 2 to 3 and saturation decreased from 0.4 to 0.2
  - Spider Meat poison ticks have been decreased from 300 ticks to 240 ticks
  - Cooked Spider Meat nutrition increased from 5 to 7 and saturation decreased from 0.7 to 0.6
  - Slime Noodles nutrition increased from 1 to 2
  - Silverfish Abdomen nutrition increased from 2 to 3 and now consumes fast
  - Ghast Tentacle nutrition increased from 3 to 4 and saturation increased from 0.3 to 0.4
  - Sniffer foods no longer have extended consuming times and base sniffer ingredients no longer harmonize effects
  - Amethyst Rock Candy is now crafted in the crafting table
  - Amethyst Rock Candy max stack size changed from 16 to 64
  - Amethyst Rock Candy nutrition increased from 4 to 6 and saturation decreased from 0.5 to 0.4
  - Amethyst Rock Candy use ticks reduced from 48 to 32
  - Amethyst Rock Candy no longer grants Strength (and thus also no longer always edible)
- Many recipes have also been updated, below is the full list:
  - Monster Pot recipe has been simplified
  - Wormroot's stick recipe now mimics Bamboo's stick recipe
  - Wormwood Plank's recipe tree now mimics Bamboo Planks's recipe tree
  - Stained Scrap Trapdoor now mimics Iron Trapdoor
  - Stained Scrap Bars now mimics Iron Bars
- Spiders now reap Cooked Spider Meat while on fire
- Zombie Villagers, Zombified Piglins, Zoglins, and Zombie Horses will now reap Rotten Tripe
- Hardness of stained blocks collectively reduced from 8 to 6
