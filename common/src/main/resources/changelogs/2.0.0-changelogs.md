## The Final Feast Update || v2.0.0 MC-1.20.1

----------------------
### As you may have guessed by the title, *this will be the final content update for Dungeon's Delight.*

We have reached the conclusion that culminating with this update we've added everything that
we wanted to *Dungeon's Delight* and most of the developers behind the mod want to focus on things beyond Minecraft modding. 
This update adds the final handful of things that we had left on the back burner (**including separating the mod from Farmer's Delight!**), 
as well as some other bits and goodies here and there.

That being said, this is no small update at all; *we've rewritten the entire mod from the ground-up to remove reliance on
Farmer's Delight (although you can still play with it and get the same experience!), **AND to make way for a Fabric port for
both 1.20.1 & 1.21.1!***

*Take a look at all the changes below!*

----------------------

### Notes
- With this update, **THE ENTIRETY OF DUNGEON'S DELIGHT HAS BEEN REWRITTEN FROM THE GROUND UP.** This means several things:
  - Dungeon's Delight no longer requires Farmer's Delight (although you should still have a similar experience to previous 
  updates if you have it installed together!)
  - **MAKE BACKUPS OF YOUR WORLDS!!!** We've done the best we can to ensure everything from prior updates works, but there's no
  guarantee that things won't break! **Consider yourself warned.**
    - With the code being based on the latest 1.21.1 version, most reasonable changes from 1.21.1 have been ported to 1.20.1; refer to version 1.3 - 1.5 changelogs to see the majority of changes
    - There may be a bunch of minor changes not noted due to rewriting everything from the ground up

*Additionally, RunicLib 4.3.9+ is now required in order to play Dungeon's Delight*

### Removals & Breaking Changes
***Most items removed will have their id migrated to the next closest thing to prevent losing a bunch of progress, updating should still be taken at a risk!***
- Removed Items
  - Brined Flesh & Gritty Flesh: these felt like "bloat" items that existing items like Rotten Tripe could already fulfill (id migrated to Rotten Flesh)
  - Slime Slab: this is for the same reason as the above but for Slime Balls and Slime Noodles (id migrated to Slime Ball)

### Rotten Farming
- Added Terror Preta, a new block used for growing Dungeon's Delight's crops
  - Requires water like normal farmland, and additionally needs to be hidden from sunlight
  - Exposing it to sunlight will cause it to burn like the undead
  - Rotten crops are allowed in any light level unlike vanilla crops

### Misc Additions
- Added Darting, a new uncommon non treasure enchantment for the cleaver
  - Has a max level of III
  - Decreases charge time by 12% per level
  - Increased throw range (I - +0.16, II - +0.33, III - +0.5)
- Added Creeperilla while in its raw form its highly volatile and will explode on impact
  - Consuming Creeperilla with active effects will cause them to leak out in lingering clouds and cause a small explosion dealing some damage

### Cleaver Overhaul
- All cleaver drops have been rebalanced (cleaver drops are also now known as "reapings")
- Thrown cleavers will now always drop reapings (no longer required to hold a cleaver the entire time before the entity dies)
- Entities will no longer drop reapings when the cleaver is not in the attacking hand
- As usual, cleavers have additionally received a few small balance tweaks, below are the changes
  - Knockback is no longer allowed on cleavers
  - Fire Aspect is now allowed on cleavers, reaped drops will be cooked (beware of the fact cooked ingredients cannot be used for cooking)
  - Serrated Strike now goes up to level 3 and is incompatible with Fire Aspect
    - Serrated Strike levels decrease the piercing damage penalty by 5% per level
    - Serrated Strike levels increase the damage rate of the Serrated debuff by increasing its amplifier (I - 30, II - 15, III - 10)
    - Serrated effect damage decreased from 2 to 1
    - Serrated effect tick rate has been increased from 20 to 30 (nerf)
    - Serrated effect no longer does more damage with each amplifier (was never obtainable in previous versions)
    - Serrated effect no longer damages through enchantments, resistance, or other potion effects
    - Serrated damage now drops cleaver reapings
    - Serrated Strike is now a rare enchantment, previously was common rarity
  - Cleavers now require a quarter of their charge to be completed before they can be thrown (8 ticks)
  - Fully charged cleavers now have halved cooldown
  - All cleavers have had their attack damage decreased by -0.5
  - All cleavers have had their attack speed increased by +0.1 (are now faster)
  - All cleavers have had their throwing range decreased by -0.5
  - Flint Cleavers have had their ranged increased by +0.16
  - Golden Cleavers have had their range increased by +0.5

### Wormouth Overhaul
Prior to this update, wormouths didn't really do a lot; they acted as a means of obtaining specific monster ingredients,
but said ingredients were already relatively easy to obtain, giving less incentive to make note of any wormouths you'd come
across. With this update, wormouths have received a fresh coat of paint:
- Wormouth drops are now entirely data-driven! This means that addons or datapackers can add their own wormouth drops:
  - Information is stored in `data/<namespace>/dungeonsdelight/wormouth`
  - Each `.json` file requires a few things:
    - Either a defined `item` or `tag` (**only one can be filled, otherwise it won't load**)
    - The loot table (`table`) to pull from when giving items
    - A boolean for whether the recipe should cause the Wormouth uses to deplete (`exhaust`)
- Wormouths can now provide different items based on what they're fed. Some examples include:
  - What happens if you feed them the Malady Music Disc? Well, why don't you go find out for yourself...
- Feeding Wormouths any items with food value still works the same, with a few changes:
  - The generic drop loot table has been reworked to include mob drops that are hard to obtain in Peaceful difficulty!
- Wormouths can have items directly inserted into them using hoppers, allowing for some (slow) automated feeding!
- When hit with a thrown Cleaver, Wormouths will panic and drop a bunch of random items
  - This will, in turn, make them shut for double the time, making this far more of a gamble than just feeding them normally!
- Added unique sounds for Wormouths & updated consumption animations
  - (Luckily, if for some odd reason you decide you want to break the Wormouth mid-animation, it'll still drop the item. Probably don't do that still)
- Now drop a small bit of EXP most of the time when feeding it
- Can now be waterlogged and rotated for the sake of Creative Mode building
- Now considered a block entity

### Gameplay Changes
- Zombie Villagers, Zombified Piglins, Zoglins, and Zombie Horses will now reap Rotten Tripe
- Spiders now reap Cooked Spider Meat while on fire
- Most tooltips have been updated to be more concise and less hand-holding
  - Effect tooltips now display their activate chances
  - Vanilla items like Golden Apples and Milk Buckets now have effect tooltips (this can be disabled)
- Hardness of stained blocks collectively reduced from 8 to 6

### Balance Changes
- Many foods in Dungeon's Delight have been rebalanced, below is the full list:
  - Rotten Tripe saturation increased from 0.05 to 0.1 and now consumes fast
  - Spider Meat nutrition increased from 2 to 3 and saturation decreased from 0.4 to 0.2
  - Spider Meat poison ticks have been decreased from 300 ticks to 240 ticks
  - Cooked Spider Meat nutrition increased from 5 to 7 and saturation decreased from 0.7 to 0.6
  - Slime Noodles nutrition increased from 1 to 2
  - Silverfish Abdomen nutrition increased from 2 to 3 and now consumes fast
  - Ghast Tentacle nutrition increased from 3 to 4 and saturation increased from 0.3 to 0.4
  - Sniffer foods no longer have extended consuming times and base sniffer ingredients no longer harmonize effects
  - Amethyst Rock Candy
    - Now crafted in the crafting table instead of Monster Pot
      - The recipe has also swapped Sweet Berries with Sugar
    - Nutrition increased from 4 to 6 and saturation decreased from 0.5 to 0.4
    - No longer grants Strength (and thus also no longer always edible)
    - Usage ticks reduced from 48 to 32
    - Pickup cooldown duration increased from 40 to 300
  - Candied Silverfish Sucker
    - Saturation increased from 0.3 to 0.6
    - Burrow Gut duration increased from 1800 ticks to 3600
    - Usage ticks reduced from 48 to 32
  - Candied Vex Sucker
    - Nutrition increased from 4 to 8 and saturation increased from 0.5 to 0.6
    - Usage ticks reduced from 48 to 32
- Many recipes have also been updated, below is the full list:
  - Monster Pot recipe has been simplified
  - Wormroot's stick recipe now mimics Bamboo's stick recipe
  - Wormwood Plank's recipe tree now mimics Bamboo Planks's recipe tree
  - Stained Scrap Trapdoor now mimics Iron Trapdoor
  - Stained Scrap Bars now mimics Iron Bars
- Many of the effects in Dungeon's Delight have been tweaked to increase usability and add some refinements
  - Foul Scent (formerly Putrid Scent)
    - Has had its name changed from Putrid Scent to Foul Scent
    - Foul Scent range increased from 12 to 32
    - Entities hostile towards Foul Scent users now move at 1.25 speed (was 1.2)
  - Ravenous Rush
    - No longer grants anything by default and has been reworked to grant additional bonuses for each active monster effect
      - Exudation's bonus increases the range of Exudation by +3.0 per Ravenous Rush amplifier
  - Tenacity
    - No longer considered a monster effect and is the normal variant of Voracity
    - Improved rate at which health regenerates based on hunger
  - Burrow Gut
  - Debridement (formerly Rotgut)
    - Has had its name changed from Rotgut to Debridement
  - Exudation
    - Causes the user to receive 1.5x increased damage while Exudation's hearts are active instead of 1.25x increased damage
    - Amplifier damage now scales by *3 instead of *2
    - Now preserves the amplifier of its normal variant when monsterizing
    - No longer pierces certain types of damage resistances
    - Can now be blocked with Blast Protection
    - All living entities can now use Exudation instead of just the player
  - Pouncing
    - Now considered the monsterized variant of Speed instead of Jump Boost
  - Voracity
    - Now considered the monsterized variant of Tenacity instead of Nourishment (this is due to the fact the mod is longer a Farmer's Delight addon)
    - Hunger bar now only drains when under the same conditions that undead mobs burn in
      - Hunger drain is now significantly higher but does not scale with amplifier
- Ghasts now reap 4-6 Ghast Tentacles with the possibility of more with looting, originally was 1-2 Ghast Tentacles

### Configuration
- Runiconfig is now used for configs instead of Forge's Config API due to the mod being MultiLoader, this shouldn't really impact much though
- Dungeon's Delight's configuration has been cleaned up and expanded on with more options
  - Client Configurations
    - Renamed spawnersEmitGreenFlames configuration to `spawners_emit_living_flames`, this additionally configures the spawning particles of Monster Spawners `(default: true)`
    - Added `monster_effect_background` configuration, Should Monster Effects have a special background? Disable if you experience issues relating to effect backgrounds (default: true)
    - Added `status_effect_tooltips` configuration, Should items display their status effects? (default: true)
    - Added `item_effect_tooltips` configuration, Should items display their on use effects? (default: true)
    - Added `vanilla_status_effect_tooltips` configuration, Should vanilla items display status effects? (default: true)
    - Added `vanilla_item_effect_tooltips` configuration, Should vanilla items display their on use effects? Some are automatically disabled when Bountiful Fares is loaded (default: true)
    - Added `show_chance_tooltips` configuration, Should items display their chance for effects? (default: true)
  - Cleaver Configurations
    - Added `cleaver_attack_damage` configuration, Sets the added attack damage of Cleavers (this value is added on top of tier attack damage, for example diamond tier has a base of 3.0 attack damage) (default: 2.0F)
    - Added `cleaver_attack_speed` configuration, Sets the melee attack speed of Cleavers (default: -3.0F)
    - Added `cleaver_piercing_damage_multiplier` configuration, The amount of multiplied damage Cleavers should deal when piercing an entity (default 0.8F)
    - Added `cleaver_miss_cooldown_ticks` configuration, How long Cleavers should go on cooldown after hitting the ground without piercing an entity, fully charged Cleavers have this value halved (default: 50)
    - Added `cleaver_darting_charge_multiplier` configuration, The percentage amount the Darting enchantment should change Cleaver charging time per enchantment level. Do not set as 0 (default: 0.12F)
    - Added `cleaver_serrated_effect_damage` configuration, The amount of damage the Serrated effect should deal to effected targets (default: 1.0F)
    - Added `cleaver_ricochet_damage_multiplier` configuration, The amount of multiplied damage Cleavers should deal when ricocheting off a block with the Ricochet enchantment (default: 1.33F)
  - Block Configurations
    - Added `item_grates_requires_sneaking_to_insert` configuration, Should sneaking be required to place an item into an item grate (default: true)
  - Item Configurations
    - Added `rock_candy_pickup_cooldown_ticks` configuration, The amount of time in ticks that rock candies should be on cooldown when imprisoning something (default: 200)
  - Effect Configurations
    - Added `foul_scent_range` configuration, The range that entities become hostile towards a user of Foul Scent (default: 32.0F)
    - Added `tenacity_interval` configuration, The rate that Tenacity heals the user, this is not any specific measure of time and increases the lower hunger the user has (default: 6)
    - Added `exudation_base_damage` configuration, The base damage of Exudation's blast (default: 12.0F)
    - Added `exudation_damage_multiplier` configuration, The multiplied amount of damage that exudation should inflict on the user (default: 1.25F)
    - Added `exudation_damage_multipler_while_hearts_active` configuration, Should Exudation's multiplied damage only occur while the player has Exudation hearts? If true it is active at all times (default: false)
    - Added `exudation_base_range` configuration, The base range of Exudation's blast (default: 10.0F)
    - Added `exudation_invulnerability_ticks` configuration, The amount of ticks that the player should be invulnerable when taking damage with Exudation, default without Exudation is 20 ticks (default: 30)
