## Dungeons & Delights Update || v2.0.0 MC-1.20.1

----------------------
### As you may have guessed by the title, *this will be the final content update for Dungeon's Delight.*

We have reached the conclusion that culminating with this update, we've added everything that
we wanted to *Dungeon's Delight* and most of the developers behind the mod want to focus on things beyond Minecraft modding. 
This update adds the final handful of things that we had left on the back burner (**including separating the mod from Farmer's Delight!**), 
as well as some other bits and goodies here and there.

That being said, this is no small update at all; *we've rewritten the entire mod from the ground-up to remove reliance on
Farmer's Delight (although you can still play with it and get the same experience!), **AND to make way for a Fabric port for
both 1.20.1 & 1.21.1!***

*Take a look at all the changes below!*

----------------------

### Notes
- With this update, **THE ENTIRETY OF DUNGEON'S DELIGHT HAS BEEN REWRITTEN FROM THE GROUND UP.** This means several things:
  - Dungeon's Delight no longer requires Farmer's Delight (although you should still have a similar experience to previous 
  updates if you have it installed together!)
  - **MAKE BACKUPS OF YOUR WORLDS!!!** We've done the best we can to ensure everything from prior updates works, but there's no
  guarantee that things won't break! **Consider yourself warned.**
    - With the code being based on the latest 1.21.1 version, most reasonable changes from 1.21.1 have been ported to 1.20.1; refer to version 1.3 - 1.5 changelogs to see the majority of changes
    - There may be a bunch of minor changes not noted due to rewriting everything from the ground up

***Additionally, RunicLib 4.3.9+ is now required in order to play Dungeon's Delight.***

### Removals & Breaking Changes
***Most of these will have their ID migrated to the next closest thing to prevent losing progress; regardless, update at your own risk!***
- Removed the following:
  - Brined & Gritty Flesh (will convert to Rotten Flesh)
    - These felt like "bloat" items that other items, such as Rotten Tripe, could replace without issues
  - Slime Slab (will convert to Slime Ball)
    - Same reason as Brined & Gritty Flesh, plus the absence of Farmer's Delight's Canvas made it hard to find a replacement material
  - Salt Soaked Stew

### Rotten Farming
- Added Morbid Mush, an undead farmland block used for growing rotten crops
  - Requires water to fertilize, and must also be hidden from sunlight
    - Exposure to sunlight will cause it to catch on fire like the undead
    - If not in natural light level 7 or lower the soil will begin to dry out
  - Unlike normal crops, rotten crops can be grown at any light level
- Rotten Crops now have age properties and will grow when in skylight level 7 or lower
- Rotten Crops now drop gunk alongside a tiny amount of their crop's respective produce
  - With this change Putrescent Carrots and Blighted Beetroots have been added (however they just drop Carrots and Beetroots)
- Rotten Crop conversion is now specifically picked out instead of any CropBlock and to compensate this change the rotting of crops is data driven
- Rotbulbs have been overhauled to both be improved and be consistent with the other new crops
  - Monster Yams have been entirely overhauled (more on that further below)
  - Max age increased from 4 to 9
  - Can only be grown on Morbid Mush like other rotten crops
  - Can no longer be bonemealed (configurable)
  - Non-undead entities that walk into Rotbulbs are now granted Putrid Scent for 400 ticks
  - Undead mobs will now run to Rotbulbs when they are not under its buffs
    - Now additionally grants Resistance to undead entities
    - Undead buffs have been increased from 300 ticks to 600 ticks
    - Undead buffs are only granted and attracted when the age is 6 or higher
      - Rotbulbs will produce fly particles to notify the player when they have become a hazard

### Cleaver Overhaul
- All cleaver drops have been completely rebalanced, and are now known as "reapings"
- Thrown cleavers will now always drop reapings, no longer requiring the player to hold the cleaver until an entity dies
  - This also means entities will no longer drop reapings when the cleaver is not in the attacking hand (offhand and killing via different weapon no longer works)
- Added Wooden Cleavers and Stone Cleavers
  - Flint Cleavers moved to Farmer's Delight integration and have been slightly buffed to be between stone and iron tier
- Cleavers now require a quarter of their charge to be completed before they can be thrown (8 ticks with +1.0 Charge Multiplier)
  - Fully charged cleavers now have halved cooldown
  - Attack damage decreased by -0.5 on all cleavers
  - Attack speed changed on various cleavers
    - Golden Cleaver attack speed increased by +0.1
    - Diamond Cleaver attack speed increased by +0.1
    - Netherite Cleaver attack speed additionally increased by +0.1
  - Added Throwing Range attribute, this affects the range of cleavers and replaces the old hardcoded values
    - Wooden Cleaver Throwing Range set to 0.6
    - Stone Cleaver Throwing Range set to 0.6
    - Iron Cleaver Throwing Range set to 0.8 (originally was 1.25)
    - Golden Cleaver Throwing Range set to 1.4 (originally was 1.75)
    - Diamond Cleaver Throwing Range set to 1.0 (originally was 1.5)
    - Netherite Cleaver Throwing Range set to 1.2 (originally was 1.75)
  - Added Charge Multiplier attribute, this affects the charge speed of cleavers
    - Wooden Cleaver Charge Multiplier set to 0.8
    - Stone Cleaver Charge Multiplier set to 0.9
    - All other cleavers have a Charge Multiplier of 1.0
  - Thrown Cleavers no longer pierce shields, instead disabling them (similarly to axes)
- Knockback enchantment is no longer allowed on cleavers
- Fire Aspect is now allowed on cleavers and will automatically smelt eligible reapings
  - Fire ticks duration doubled when fully charged
  - As a quality of life feature most smelted reapings can now be used in recipes
- Serrated Strike now has a max level of III, and has received the following changes:
  - Is incompatible with Fire Aspect
  - Damage decreased from 2 to 1
    - Serrated no longer does more damage with each amplifier (not that this was actually obtainable in previous versions)
    - Serrated no longer damages through armor, enchantments, Resistance, or other potion effects
  - Serrated tick rate increased from 20 to 30 (meaning it takes longer to deal damage)
    - Higher levels increase the damage rate of the Serrated debuff by increasing its amplifier (I - 30, II - 15, III - 10)
  - Serrated can no longer be applied with melee hits
    - Serrated ticks on thrown hit decreased from 40 to 20 and no longer scales with enchantment level
    - Serrated added ticks on thrown hit when fully charged changed from 30 to 50
  - Now decreases the piercing damage penalty by 4% per level
  - Serrated now allows reapings to drop
  - Rarity increased from common to rare
- Ricochet has been slightly tweaked to be more practical
  - Now has an assist when ricocheting (bounces towards a nearby target)
    - Ricochet bounces cannot target the same entity twice in a row but this can be reset once it targets a new entity, does not require to be hit (per cleaver instance)
    - Ricochet bounces cannot target players, tamed mobs, neutral mobs (while they are non-hostile to the owner), and Endermen (via `#dungeonsdelight:ricochet_cannot_target`)
  - Ricochet damage multiplier per bounce decreased from +33% damage to -8% damage
- Added Darting, a new uncommon non-treasure enchantment for the cleaver
    - Has a max level of III
    - Decreases charge time by 12% per level
    - Increases throw range (I - +0.14, II - +0.28, III - +0.42)

### Wormouth Overhaul
Prior to this update, wormouths didn't really do a lot; they acted as a means of obtaining specific monster ingredients,
but said ingredients were already relatively easy to obtain, giving less incentive to make note of any wormouths you'd come
across. With this update, wormouths have received a fresh coat of paint:
- Wormouth drops are now entirely data-driven! This means that addons or datapackers can add their own wormouth drops:
  - Information is stored in `data/<namespace>/dungeonsdelight/wormouth`
  - Each `.json` file requires a few things:
    - Either a defined `item` or `tag` (**only one can be filled, otherwise it won't load**)
    - The loot table (`table`) to pull from when giving items
    - A boolean for whether the recipe should cause the Wormouth uses to deplete (`exhaust`)
- Wormouths can now provide different items based on what they're fed. Some examples include:
  - What happens if you feed them the Malady Music Disc? Well, why don't you go find out for yourself...
- Feeding Wormouths any items with food value still works the same, with a few changes:
  - The generic drop loot table has been reworked to include mob drops that are hard to obtain in Peaceful difficulty!
- Wormouths can have items directly inserted into them using hoppers, allowing for some (slow) automated feeding!
- When hit with a thrown Cleaver, Wormouths will panic and drop a bunch of random items
  - This will, in turn, make them shut for double the time, making this far more of a gamble than just feeding them normally!
- Added unique sounds for Wormouths & updated consumption animations
  - (Luckily, if for some odd reason you decide you want to break the Wormouth mid-animation, it'll still drop the item. Probably don't do that still)
- Now drop a small bit of EXP most of the time when feeding it
- Can now be waterlogged and rotated for the sake of Creative Mode building
- Now considered a block entity

- Most tooltips have been updated to be more concise and less hand-holding

- Hardness for all Stained Scrap blocks reduced from 8 to 6
- Hardness for all Wormwood blocks changed to be consistent with other woodsets
