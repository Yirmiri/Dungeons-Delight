## The Final Feast Update || v2.0.0 MC-1.20.1 + MC-1.21.1

----------------------
### As you may have guessed by the title, *this will be the final major update to Dungeon's Delight.*

(This isn't a _Terraria_ situation where I'll say this and then spend 10 years making "final" updates - this is it!)

I and Betwixer have reached the conclusion that - culminating with this update - we've added everything that
we wanted to do *Dungeon's Delight*. This update adds the final handful of things that we had left on the back burner (**including
separating the mod from Farmer's Delight!**), as well as some other bits and goodies here and there.

That being said, this is no small update at all; *we've rewritten the entire mod from the ground-up to remove reliance on
Farmer's Delight (although you can still play with it and get the same experience!), **AND to make way for a Fabric port for
both 1.20.1 & 1.21.1!***

*Take a look at all the changes below!*

----------------------

### Notes
- With this update, **THE ENTIRETY OF DUNGEON'S DELIGHT HAS BEEN REWRITTEN FROM THE GROUND UP.** This means several things:
  - Dungeon's Delight no longer requires Farmer's Delight (although you should still have a similar experience to previous 
  updates if you have it installed together!)
  - **MAKE BACKUPS OF YOUR WORLDS!!!** We've done the best we can to ensure everything from prior updates works, but there's no
  guarantee that things won't break! **Consider yourself warned.**
  - This update additionally ports most changes from 1.21.1 versions to 1.20.1 so if interested I'd recommend reading back onto those changelogs for more information

### Removals & Breaking Changes

### Additions
- Added Morbid Mush, this is a new block that can hold Dungeon's Delight's crop onto it, it requires water like normal farmland, but additionally it needs to be hidden from sunlight, or it will burn like the undead

### Wormouth Overhaul

### Changes
- As usual cleavers have received a few balance changes, below are the changes:
  - All cleavers have had their attack damage decreased by -0.5
  - All cleavers have had their throwing range decreased by -0.25
  - Golden Cleavers have had their range increased from 1.75 to 2.0
- Many Foods in Dungeon's Delight have been rebalanced, below are the changes:
  - Spider Meat nutrition increased from 2 to 3 and saturation decreased from 0.4 to 0.3
  - Spider Meat's poison ticks have been decreased from 300 ticks to 240 ticks
  - Cooked Spider Meat nutrition increased from 5 to 8 and saturation increased from 0.7 to 0.8

### Tweaks
- Many textures have received some updates, below are the ones that were changed:
  - Updated texture of all cleavers
  - Tweaked texture of Spider Meat and Cooked Spider Meat
  - Tweaked texture of the Serrated effect
- Flint Cleavers now use a "dull" throw sound
  - This sound is determined by an item tag known as `dungeonsdelight:uses_dull_cleaver_sound`, this tag exists for addons and modpacks

### Localization
- Updated wording of many subtitles

### Fixes
- Fixed throw range tooltip of cleavers sometimes being incorrectly ordered

### Technical
- Updated the item id of `dungeonsdelight:smoked_spider_meat` to `dungeonsdelight:cooked_spider_meat`
- The throwing range of cleavers are now based on a new attribute known as `dungeonsdelight:cleaver.throwing_range`
- Added `dungeonsdelight:cleaver_mineable` block tag, this determines what cleavers can efficiently mine (replaces previously used `farmersdelight:knife_mineable`)
- Updated id of `dungeonsdelight:flaming_knives` item tag to `dungeonsdelight:flaming_cleavers`
