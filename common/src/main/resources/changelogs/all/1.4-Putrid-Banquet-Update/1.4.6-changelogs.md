## Putrid Banquet Patch || v1.4.6 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Additions
- When Living Fire blocks steal exp points it now stores it inside with a cap of 30 levels, when broken is drops 75% of the exp points
- Living Fire blocks now steal 1 exp point at a fast rate instead of 3 exp points every 1.5 seconds
- Added new death message for dying to Living Fire or Living Campfires
- Added Rot and Steel, can be used to place Living Fire on any block, when used on Soul Sand it will create Spirit Fire

### Changes
- Fixed Cleavers dealing 1.5x more damage than they should be doing
    - Due to these changes the attack damage of Cleavers have increased by +0.5
    - Cleavers now deal 1.5x increased damage when fully charged instead of +0.75
- All foods that grant some sort of effect are always edible instead of only when hungry
- Biteable foods no longer are required for eat all advancements

### Recipe Changes
- Stained Scrap Block now requires x2 Stained Scrap Fragments and x2 Stained Scrap instead of x4 Stained Scrap and now outputs x4 instead of x1
- Stained Doors and Trapdoors now require Stained Scrap Fragments instead of Stained Scrap
- Stained Lantern now outputs x4 instead of x1 and is cheaper on Stained Scrap Fragments
- Living Torch now outputs x16 instead of x4
- Living Candle now outputs x4 instead of x1 and requires Stained Scrap Fragment instead of Stained Scrap
- Living Lantern now outputs x4 instead of x1 and is cheaper on Stained Scrap Fragments
- Living Campfire now requires Stained Scrap Fragment instead of Stained Scrap
- Fixed Living Jack o'Lantern requiring a normal Rotgourd instead of Carved Rotgourd

### Tweaks
- Updated the textures/palette of all Stained Scrap items and blocks
- Updated the texture of Rotgourds

### Localization
- Updated the description of the "Free Spirit" advancement

### Fixes
- As said above fixed Cleavers dealing 1.5x more damage than they should be doing
- Echo Blast particles from Sculk foods now properly render without needing to look down
- Fixed Living Campfires dropping more Stained Scrap than they were made of
- Fixed both eat all advancements requiring food items not present in the mod
- Fixed double tooltip on sippable items
