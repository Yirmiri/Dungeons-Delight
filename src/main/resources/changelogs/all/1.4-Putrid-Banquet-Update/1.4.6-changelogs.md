## Putrid Banquet Patch || v1.4.6 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod!
  You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/
- Plans to update the wiki will come up soon, just been busy!

### Additions
- When Living Fire blocks steal exp points it now stores it inside with a cap of 30 levels, when broken is drops half the exp points
- Living Fire blocks now steal 2 exp points at a fast rate instead of 3 exp points every 1.5 seconds
- Added new death message for dying to Living Fire or Living Campfires

### Recipe Changes
- Stained Scrap Block now requires x2 Stained Scrap Fragments and x2 Stained Scrap instead of x4 Stained Scrap and now outputs x4 instead of x1
- Stained Doors and Trapdoors now require Stained Scrap Fragments instead of Stained Scrap
- Stained Lantern now outputs x4 instead of x1 and is cheaper on Stained Scrap Fragments
- Living Torch now outputs x16 instead of x4
- Living Candle now outputs x4 instead of x1 and requires Stained Scrap Fragment instead of Stained Scrap
- Living Lantern now outputs x4 instead of x1 and is cheaper on Stained Scrap Fragments
- Living Campfire now requires Stained Scrap Fragment instead of Stained Scrap
- Fixed Living Jack o'Lantern requiring a normal Rotgourd instead of Carved Rotgourd

### Fixes
- Echo Blast particles from Sculk foods now properly render without needing to look down
- Fixed Living Campfires dropping more Stained Scrap than they were made of
