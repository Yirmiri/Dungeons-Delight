- Dungeon's Delight's first ever music disc - composed by @TheArtyrian!
- Very rarely found in Rotten Monster Rooms

### Additions
- Added two new configurations for Rotten Monster Room in and out of Swamp spawn chances

### Changes
- Rotten Monster Rooms chance has been decreased
    - 40% in swamps -> 25% in swamps
    - 25% otherwise -> 5% otherwise
- "Weird" Rotten Monster Rooms chance has been decreased
    - 1/10 in swamps -> 1/50
    - 1/20 otherwise -> 1/30
