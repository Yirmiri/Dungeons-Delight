## Putrid Banquet Patch || v1.4.2 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod!
  You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Additions
- Added Necronog, doubles the duration of a random effect that is above level 1 but decreases its level by 1
- Due to conflicts with other mods, Rotten Monster Rooms are now a seperate feature from regular Monster Rooms

### Tweaks
- Tweaked textures of all Cleavers
- Tweaked textures of both Spider Meats
- Tweaked the spawn rates of Rotten Monster Rooms once more
- Updated texture of Malady Music Disc

### Fixes
- Fixed `#boats` entity tag breaking
- Fixed Ravenous Rush overlay appearing while the game is paused
- Blocks in `dungeonsdelight:monster_heat_sources` are now apart of `dungeonsdelight:base_living_fire_blocks`
- Fixed ID issue with Rotten Dungeon spawn rates
- Removed Fried Wormroot (was never supposed to make it into full release, my apologies)

### Integration
