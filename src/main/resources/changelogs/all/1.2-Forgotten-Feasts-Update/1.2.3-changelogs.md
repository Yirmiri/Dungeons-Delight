## Forgotten Frights Update || v1.2.3 Forge-1.20.1

### Notes
- We now have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

- With the many overhauls, tweaks, and introduced missing content brought into the Forgotten Fright updates I will now shift
my focus towards NeoForge/Fabric 1.21.1 versions. Bug fixes, tweaks, and translations will be pushed out if required and if I have time.

### Additions
- Added Rotten Zombies, these replace the normal Zombies spawned by Monster Yam and are slightly stronger but 
drop Gunk and rarely Rotbulbs
- Rotbulb Crops will now drop Monster Yam loot if the difficulty is on peaceful to prevent the inability to obtain Rotbulbs
- Added 2 new splash texts

### Removals
- Removed the Persistence enchantment - This decision was made due to its cooldown refreshing only being useful in very niche
situations, and it's slowing effects being very powerful

### Changes
- Stained Knife had it's attack damage changed from 5.5 -> 5.0
- Stained Cleaver had it's attack damage changed from 5.5 -> 6.5
- Monster Yams are no immune to suffocation
- Bubblegunk now reduces hunger by -6 rather than -2, this hopefully opens up more usage cases with Tenacity
- Wormouths will now enjoy a wider variety of items, mostly satiating or complex monster foods
- Wormouths will now have a chance to drop 2-3 Stained Scrap rather than 1-2 Stained Scrap Fragments when food they enjoy
- Wormroot Stalks can now ignite on fire
- Snuffledog now requires Raw Snufflewurst instead of Cooked Snufflewurst to keep inline with other meat requiring foods

### Tweaks
- Updated the texture of Living Fire (@slimeslabs)
- Tweaked the palette of living flame blocks to align with experience more
- Slime foods are now ordered below Spider foods in the creative inventory
- Moved where biteable foods are found in the creative inventory (pretty much just Bubblegunk and compat stuff at the moment though lmao)
- Monster Yam summon particles have been changed to living flames to match their lore purposes
- Monster Yam Spawn Egg is now monster rarity
- The Corpsebloom advancement description has been tweaked to "Plant a rotbulbling, a disgusting plant that spells like death"
to better show to the player that Rotbulbs attract undead mobs and to hide the surprise of them popping out a Monster Yam
- Sculk Polyps are no longer required for the all mob knife drops advancement due to it dropping from the Warden being
a very small secret and not the intended way of obtaining them.

### Translations
- [Note] Due to code changes translations for Rotbulbling and Wild Rotbulb items have broken
- Translations have been updated for ru_ru (Credits: @Serperch)
- Translations have been updated for zh_tw (Credits: @DEEMsss)

### Technical
- Updated the item tag id `wormmouth_blacklisted`'s to `wormouth_blacklisted`
- Added item tag `wormouth_favorites`, when items in this tag are also in `monster_foods`, have food properties, and are 
fed to Wormouth's they will drop their liked foods loot table

### Fixes
- Fixed Allays not accepting Amethyst Shards for duplication (...for real this time)
- Fixed Rotbulblings and Wild Rotbulbs composting not working
- Fixed Biteable foods being enchantable
- Fixed Bubblegunk being capable of causing negative hunger points
- Fixed Wormouths being pushable by Pistons
- Fixed Rancid Reduction being required for advancements that require eating all types of a food (this was a leftover from
when Rancid Reduction was a consumable)
- Fixed missing tooltip effect tooltip on Shiokara
