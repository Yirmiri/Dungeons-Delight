### Rotgourds & Rotgut Effect
- Added Rotgourd, a new pure rot crop ready for your tending
    - Can be obtained by splashing a Pumpkin with Rancid Reduction
    - Can be carved into a Carved Rotgourd using Shears, Knives, or a Cleaver
- Added Rotgut as Rotgourd's Monster Effect
    - Monsterizes Regeneration
    - When damaged while this effect is active a portion of lost health will be turned into rotten hearts (cap of +8 rotten hearts +2 per level of effect)
    - Rotten hearts will not protect any damage and will act like empty containers
    - When dealing damage to entities with rotten hearts half the damage dealt will be siphoned back into normal health
    - When damage is dealt with rotten hearts there will always be a minimum of 1 heart that heals in the case the player deals less than 2 damage
    - A new advancement has been added for obtaining Rotgut
- Added Spider Bisque, Grants Rotgut I for 2:00 and Pouncing I for 2:00
- Added Rotpop, grants Rotgut I for 1:30 and can be consumed quickly
- Added Rot Roast, grants Rotgut II for 1:30, Burrow Gut II for 1:30, and 75% of the time Ravenous Rush I for 0:10, can be bitten multiple times
- Added Putrid Spice Latte, grants Rotgut III for 2:30
- Tokayaki and Ghast Rolls are now crafted with Rotgourd Slices instead of Rotbulbs and grant Rotgut instead of Exudation

### Sculk Foods Overhaul (Echo Blast)
- Majority of Sculk Foods now unleash an Echo Blast that causes low damage but high knockback, these are helpful for getting out of
a situation or attempting to eat foods while leaving room for Exudation to deal with tanking and offensive situations
- Sculk Foods have 3 varying levels of blast size, each has a longer lifespan, windup time, and range
- Sculk Foods that have an Echo Blast are now always edible
- The level is determined by how many sculk ingredients are in an item's recipe
- Echo Blasts can only affect hostile mobs
- Echo Blasts inflict Slowness for 0:02
- The `#ignores_echo_blast` entity tag has been added for mobs that should be unaffected, by default this includes Ender Dragon, Wither, and Warden
- Some foods no longer have any effect which includes:
  - Wardenzola
  - Wardenzola Crumbles
  - Sculk Mayo
  - Cleaved Ancient Egg
  - Echo Rock Candy
- Sculk Tarts, Polterghast Pizza, and Echo Rock Candy will keep the experience effect
- Devilish Eggs are now eaten quickly
- Gyudon now can monsterize an effect
- Poisonous Poutine now costs an extra `#sculk_cheese` item
- Echo Rock Candy now grants 50% more experience
- Echo Rock Candy Strength II duration reduced from 1:30 to 0:45

### Sniffer Foods Overhaul (Harmonizing)
- Foods created using Sniffer meat now has a chance to cure a Monster Effect, this hopefully makes them feel connected to the mod rather
than a random addition
  - Raw Snifferwurst has a 10% chance
  - Cooked Snifferwrust has a 15% chance
  - Soft Served Sniffer Egg has a 50% chance
  - Chloropasta's has a 20% chance
  - Snuffledog has a 100% chance
- Sniffer foods no longer have slower eating speed
- Cooked Sniffer Shank saturation decreased from 0.9 to 0.8
- Raw Snifferwurst and Cooked Snifferwurst can no longer grant Regeneration
- Raw Snifferwurst saturation increased from 0.5 to 0.6
- Cooked Snifferwurst saturation increased from 0.7 to 0.8
- Soft Served Sniffer Egg saturation increased from 0.4 to 0.6
- Soft Served Sniffer Egg stack sized increased from 16 to 64
- Snuffledog saturation decreased from 1.1 to 1.0
- Snuffledog Regeneration level decreased from II to I
- Chloropasta chance to not be consumed increased from 10% to 20%
- Removed Ominous Omelette item
- Advancement for eating a Sniffer Food has been updated to curing an effect using Sniffer Food

### Rotten Zombies Overhaul (Zombified Dryads)
- Rotten Zombies have been renamed to Zombified Dryads
- Zombified Dryads now have a new texture & sounds (Sound credit: @TheArtyrian)
- Zombified Dryads spawn with a Rotgourd equipped 25% of the time, while worn if hurt by a Knife or Cleaver the Rotgourd will be
  split open and drop Rotgourd Slices, a new advancement has been added alongside this interaction
- Zombified Dryads with Living Jack o'Lantern's equipped will also be granted permanent Rotgut until the Rotgourd is removed

### Glow Berry Gelatin Overhaul (Monster Mousse)
- Glow Berry Gelatin has been renamed to Monster Mousse (this will break existing Glow Berry Gelatin in worlds)
- Texture has been updated to match the name
- Monster Mousse grants Tenacity instead of Comfort and additionally Exudation
- Recipe has been updated to use a Rotbulb instead of a Glow Berry
- Monster Mousse is now considered a Monster Food
- This change alongside Sniffer foods hopefully means all foods in the mod should feel true to the theme

### Cleavers Balancing
- Cleavers now increase in velocity when charging up, charging up is optional but fully charging a Cleaver will be rewarded
  - Charging a Cleaver to 75% will be the same as how it used to be in previous versions
  - Fully charged Cleavers will decrease the miss cooldown time by 50%
  - Fully charged Cleavers will deal +0.75 additional damage
  - Fully charged Cleavers will increase the time of Serrated inflicted on entities
  - A "shine" will now play when the Cleaver is fully charged (Sound credit: @TheArtyrian)
- Cleaver charge levels
  - 100% charged is 32 ticks
  - 75% charged is 24 ticks
  - 50% charged is 16 ticks
  - 25% charged is 8 ticks
- All Cleavers have had their Throw Range changed
  - Flint Cleaver: 1.25x -> 1.0x
  - Iron Cleaver: 1.5x -> 1.25x
  - Golden Cleaver: Unchanged (1.75x)
  - Diamond Cleaver: 1.75x -> 1.5x
  - Netherite Cleaver: Unchanged (1.75x)
- All Cleavers now are given a cooldown when a thrown is missed
- All Cleavers have had their attack speed decreased by 0.1
- Thrown Cleaver damage bonus has been decreased from +1.66 to +1.5
- Serrated damage tick rate has been decreased from 20 ticks to 10 ticks
- Serrated no longer bypasses Resistance
- Throw Range tooltip now displays as a multiplier rather than a fixed number

### Rotten Dungeons (thanks to @TheArtyrian)
- Generation code has been completely overhauled
  - Instead of a seperate structure, Rotten Dungeons now share generation with regular Monster Rooms. They will now have a 10% chance of replacing them upon generation
  - Rotten Dungeons will no longer all look the same; they'll have randomized layouts every time, just like regular Monster Rooms!
  - This overhaul fixes several issues with previous Rotten Dungeons:
    - Now that the code is shared with Monster Rooms, you'll FINALLY be able to see them exposed to caves, making them far easier to find!
    - Rotten Dungeons will no longer spawn in awkward locations, i.e the ocean, surface, or air
