## Tenacious Garden Update || v1.5.0 NeoForge-1.21.1

**This update adds compatibility with Farmer's Delight 1.3.0+ and is also thus now the minimum required 
version to play Dungeon's Delight. Additionally, NeoForge v21.1.219 or later is required.**

*TLDR: Farmer's Delight v1.3+ and NeoForge 21.1.219+ are required*

### Changes
- Updated mod description

### Fixes
- Fixed many issues involving Rot and Steel
  - Rot and Steel is now tagged with `#minecraft:durability_enchantable` (allows it to have enchantments such as Unbreaking or Mending)
  - Rot and Steel can now ignite Stoves, TNT, and Creepers
  - Fixed missing Dispenser functionality for Rot and Steel
- Fixed missing Dispenser functionality for Gunk Arrows
