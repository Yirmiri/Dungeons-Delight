## Final Fright Update || v1.4.0 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Notes
- Versioning has been slightly reformatted, all future updates will now follow a semantic versioning scheme

### Additions
- Added Rotgourd, can be obtained by splashing a Pumpkin with Rancid Reduction
- Added 2 new splash texts

### Tweaks
- Rancid Reduction now has a particle trail when thrown
- Cleavers now use the Trident throw animation when in 3rd person
- A few of the cleaver sounds now have random pitch
- Slightly lowered the volume of the Cleaver
- Tweaked Rotbulbing item texture
- Renamed "Cold Winds" advancement to "Ice Scream"

### Translations
- Translations have been updated for `zh_cn`, Simplified Chinese (Credits: @IwasConfused)

### Fixes
- Fixed the inability to get the "Monsters Smashed" advancement
- Removed "Slice of Life" advancement
- Fixed Wormwood Door incorrectly tagged as `wooden_cabinets` instead of Wormwood Cabinet
- Fixed incorrect Rotbulbing item texture being used (somewhere in the 1.21.1 port an older version was moved in I guess)
