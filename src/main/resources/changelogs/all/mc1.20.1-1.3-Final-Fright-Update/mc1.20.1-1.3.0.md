  You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Note
- This update will be referred to as mc1.20.1-v1.3.0 as it is different from 1.21.1's v1.3
- This update brings over various QoL, fixes, and reworks from 1.21.1's v1.3 and v1.4 versions however most content has been left out

### Additions
- Added many splash texts

### Changes
- Removed Ominous Omelette
- Monster Yam now drop additional Rotbulbs and are affected by Looting
- Wardenzola & Wardenzola Crumbles are no longer considered a Monster Food
  - Additionally, no longer requires Rotbulb to be crafted
- Devilish Eggs, Spider Donuts, and Monster Muffins are now crafted in pairs of x2
- Taro Milk Tea no longer requires Honey Bottle to be crafted
- Ossobuco recipe updated to use extra Bone and Rotten Tomato instead of Beetroot and Bone Broth

### Tweaks
- Updated all existing Stained Scrap textures
- Updated Cob n' Candy texture
- Tweaked Rotbulbling texture
- Updated name of `feed_wormouth` advancement
- Advancements for obtaining effects no longer appear in the chat

### Fixes
- Fixed Sculk Polyp's being unstackable
- Cleaned up recipe errors spamming logs
- Renamed "Wormwood Chest Boat" to "Wormwood with Chest Boat" to maintain consistency with vanilla (does not affect item id)
- Wormwood Cabinets are now a part of Farmer's Delight's cabinet tag rather than Wormwood Door
- Hoss.png has run away
