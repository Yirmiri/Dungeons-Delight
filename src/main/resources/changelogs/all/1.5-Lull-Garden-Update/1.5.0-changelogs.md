## Lull Garden Update || v1.5.0 NeoForge-1.21.1

**This update adds compatibility with Farmer's Delight 1.3.0+ and is also thus now the minimum required 
version to play Dungeon's Delight. Additionally, NeoForge v21.1.219 or later is required.**

*There will be some changes to go along with Farmer's Delight 1.3 such as Tenacity being moved away from Comfort, these changes will come in a future release however*

*TLDR: Farmer's Delight v1.3+ and NeoForge 21.1.219+ are required*

### Changes
- Ricochet now increases Cleaver damage by 1.33x for each consecutive bounce (previously 1.25x)
- Gunk Arrows not inflict Putrid Scent for 15 seconds (previously 10 seconds)
- Chloropasta now grants Nourishment instead of Comfort
- Reduced use time on Bubblegunk to 12 ticks (previously 24 ticks)
- Rich Soil now influences Rotbulb growth
- Raw Spider Meat can now be smelted and campfire cooked into Smoked Spider Meat (previously exclusive to Smokers)
- Ghast Calamari can now be smelted and campfire cooked into Fried Calamari (previously exclusive to Smokers)
- Sniffer Egg can now only be campfire cooked into Soft Serve Sniffer Egg (previously exclusive to Smokers)
- Dungeon Stoves now only drain experience in a 13x radius on the top of the block
