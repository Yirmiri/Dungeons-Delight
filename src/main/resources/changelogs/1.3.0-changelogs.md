## Final Feast Update || v1.3.0 Forge-1.20.1

### Notes
- This likely will be my last content update for the time being while I go and work on new projects beyond Minecraft,
this mod will still be maintained and be ported to 1.21.1 (in the future maybe we will see don't count on it soon :tm:)

### Additions
- Added Creeperilla, a new ingredient that can be obtained by defeating a Creeper with a Knife or Cleaver
- Added Creeperilla Squib, the cut variant of Creeperilla, a primary ingredient for many foods but must be used quickly, or it will explode!
- Added various new foods to go along with the new Creeper ingredients (for further information visit their pages at https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/)

### Changes
- Stained Knife had it's attack damage changed from 5.5 -> 5.0
- Stained Cleaver had it's attack damage changed from 5.5 -> 6.5
- Snuffledog now requires Raw Snufflewurst to keep inline with other meat requiring foods

### Tweaks
- Updated the texture of Living Fire (@slimeslabs)
- Tweaked the palette of living flame blocks to align with experience more
- Slime foods are now ordered below Spider foods in the creative inventory
- Moved where biteable foods are found in the creative inventory (pretty much just Bubblegunk and compat stuff at the moment though lmao)

### Translations
- Translations have been updated for ru_ru, Russian (Credits: @Serperch)

### Fixes
- Fixed Allays not accepting Amethyst Shards for duplication... for real this time
- Fixed Rancid Reduction being required for advancements that require eating all types of a food (this is a leftover from v1.0)
- Shiokara now displays it's food effect tooltip
