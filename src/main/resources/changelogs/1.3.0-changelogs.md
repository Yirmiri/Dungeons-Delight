## Tasty Trials Update || v1.3.0 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Notes
- [Important] Stained Cleavers & Stained Knives have temporarily been hidden and unobtainable in Survival, this is due to my
dissatisfaction with their usages, I likely will either bring them back or replace them in the future, but I need time to concept
for them before making any decisions.

### Additions
- Ported the mod to version NeoForge 1.21.1
- Added Swift Step monster effect, it's normal variant is Speed
  - Grants the ability to dash when sneaking midair but only refreshes after 20 ticks and landing on the ground
  - Higher levels of the effect grants more dashes before needing to touch the ground
  - Dashes will also push back entities, higher levels of the effect will increase the radius
  - After dashing weakness will be applied for 5 seconds unless the user has Ravenous Rush
  - If Ravenous Rush is currently active dashes will instead refresh every 10 ticks
  - Fall damage might cause you a bit of a headache so consider using Pouncing to decrease fall damage taken
- Added Wispy Rice Ball, grants Swift Step for 3:00 and has a 15% chance to not be consumed
- Added Breeze Cream Cone, grants Swift Step II for 1:30, has a 15% chance to not be consumed, and extinguishes fire but causes freezing to the consumer
- Added Marshbellow, grants Swift Step for 1:30, has a 25% chance to not be consumed, and is quick to consume
- Added Jelly Beans, can be eaten quickly and grants 2 nutrition and 0.1 saturation, when eaten it will grant either a random
positive or random negative effect, some of these effects are uniquely obtained only from the Jelly Beans
- Added Polterghast Pizza, grants Exudation for 2:00 and Ravenous Rush for 0:05
- Added Echo Rock Candy, grants Perception V and Blindness for 3:00 and Strength II for 1:30, is a Biteable food that can be chewed 16 times,
when the food is chewed while Blindness is active it will clear Perception and Blindness
- Added Gluttony Pottery Sherd, these can be found in Trial Chambers
- Trial Spawners and Vaults are now valid Monster Pot heat sources and also have Living Flame particles
- [Experimental (may revert)] Biteable foods can now be stacked, when they are split they all are given the durability of the bitten food
  but when combined and consumed it will decrease 1 stack and reset the durability, stacks with different durability cannot be combined
- Added 2 new splash texts

### Changes
- Cleavers now deal knockback when hitting an entity
- Life Grasp enchantment can no longer be traded for
- Monster Effects now display a special ambient texture
- Exudation now displays a custom heart icon
- Burrow Gut and Voracity now display custom hunger icons
- Burrow Gut and Voracity's hunger exhaustion has been reduced from 0.075 to 0.05
- Pouncing effect now adds 0.15 sneaking speed per level
- Most foods that do not give a monster effect or have a purple name are now made in Farmer's Delight's normal cooking pot
- Foods that required cooked variants in their monster cooking recipes now required the raw variant
- All foods that grant experience points have had their experience payout doubled (this is experimental, so if possible feedback would be nice)
- Monster Muffins now output x2 when cooked
- Spider Donuts now output x2 when cooked
- Devilish Eggs now output x2 when cooked
- All Sculk foods now grant x2 the experience of what their food type normally would give
- Ossobuco now takes 400 ticks to cook rather than 200 ticks
- Rubaboo now takes 200 ticks to cook rather than 100 ticks
- Rubaboo no longer can be made using Honey Bottles
- Bubblegunk no longer requires Rotbulb to craft
- Cob n' Candy can no longer be made using Cobwebs however the String recipe has been reduced from x5 String to x3 String
- Tweaked the recipe of Ominous Omelette to now take x1 of each mushroom rather than any of x1
- Biteable foods can now always be eaten instead of only when hungry
- Glowberry Gelatin has had it's Perception duration decreased by 50%
- Rubaboo is now a Biteable food, it's effect durations have been decreased by 50%
- Au Rotten Potatoes are now a Biteable food, it's effect duration and experience output have been decreased by 50%
- Cob n' Candy have had it's effect durations increased by 55%
- Bubblegunk now takes 24 ticks to consume rather than 32 ticks
- Bubblegunk will now only reduce 2 hunger when sneaking
- Embedded Eggs now cost 5 eggs but drop 5 Ancient Eggs
- Rotten Zombies no longer burn in the sunlight

### Tweaks

- A new particle will appear when thrown Rancid Reduction hits a block
- Updated the texture of Cob n' Candy
- Biteable foods now only play the eat sound upon fully consuming a Biteable food
- Any instance of "...can be eaten multiple times..." has been changed to "...can be chewed multiple times..."
- Pushed the "Throw Range" tooltip on Cleavers up 1 line

- Fixed an issue with the Bloody Mary item not converting Haste into Burrow Gut
- Fixed Pouncing not decreasing fall damage on non-player entities
- Brined Flesh and Gritty Flesh can now be fed to Wolves
- Fixed Sculk Polyps not stacking
- Fixed a crash with Foxes consuming Bubblegunk
- Added missing pastry slicing loot tables for Spider Pie, Monster Cake and Monster Cake with Candle
