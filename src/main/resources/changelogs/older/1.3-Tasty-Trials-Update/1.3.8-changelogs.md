## Tasty Trials Patch || v1.3.8 NeoForge-1.21.1

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Additions
- Cleavers can now chop thrown Ancient Eggs midair, a new advancement has been attached with it

### Changes
- Candied Vex Sucker no longer grants Speed due to monsterized Speed existing, as compensation to allow it to keep up it now
grants Decisive II instead of just Decisive I
- Updated the texture of Cleaved Ancient Egg to center the texture better
- Increased the size of thrown Ancient Egg's hitbox from 0.25 to 0.5
- Updated the advancement icon of "Paint the Town Blue" from Cleaved Ancient Egg to Wardenzola

### Tweaks
- Monster Pot heat tooltip has been changed to mention "living heat" rather than just "heat"
- Cob n' Candy now evaporates in water

### Fixes
- Fixed Living Candles being unable to be unlit
- `obtain_stained_weapon` advancement has temporarily been removed while Stained Knives & Stained Cleavers remain out of the mod
- Campfire cooking recipes now have the suffix `_from_campfire` instead of mistakenly `_from_blasting`

## Integration
### Additions
- [Aether] Added integration with Aether to add new foods, cleavers, and knives (Zanite Knife recipe is diagonal due to Nature Staff recipe)
- [Twilight Forest] Added Maze Smore, grants Swift Step for 1:00 and can be eaten fast

### Changes
- [Miner's Delight] Updated integration textures
- [Twilight Forest] All TF Spawner blocks now heat the Monster Pot

### Tweaks
- "Dungeon's Delight: Compatibility" creative tab has been renamed to "Dungeon's Delight: Integration" as that would be 
the proper word to describe its usage

### Fixes
- [Miner's Delight] Fixed insane saturation on Copper Cup foods
- [Twilight Forest] Fixed missing recipes for most items
- [Twilight Forest] Fixed TF cleavers & knives being stackable
- [Twilight Forest] Fixed TF cleavers missing the `cleavers` item tag
- [Twilight Forest] Fixed TF knives missing the `knives` item tag
- [Twilight Forest] Fixed TF Monster Foods missing the `monster_foods` item tag
