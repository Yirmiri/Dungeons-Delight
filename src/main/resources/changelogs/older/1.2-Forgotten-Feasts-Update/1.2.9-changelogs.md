## Forgotten Frights Patch || v1.2.9 Forge-1.20.1

### Note
- Minimum RunicLib version to run this mod has been changed from 4.1.5 to 4.3.7

### Wiki Reminder
- We have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

### Additions
- Added Rubaboo, grants Tenacity for 1:00 and Fire Resistance for 2:00, this food has many different ways of making it
- Added Wormwood Boats and Wormwood Chest Boats
- Added 5 new advancements
  - "...And I'm All Out of Gum" // Fully consume a Bubblegunk
  - "FEED US WE WILL GROW" // Feed a Wormouth a food item
  - "Air Pollution" // Shoot a Gunk Arrow
  - "Septic Tank" // Shoot a Gunk Arrow at a Monster Yam and have its own horde attack it
  - "Choking Hazard" // Eat a Slime Food and have it not be consumed

### Changes
- All foods that require Brined Flesh or Gritty Flesh are given Water Breathing and Fire Resistance respectively if they didn't already (excluding Bloody Mary)
  - Terrine Loaf now grants Water Breathing for 1:30
  - Gyudon now grants Fire Resistance for 1:30
- Added a tooltip to Gunk Arrows
- Updated the name of the "Dice Roller" advancement to "Chance Roll", this is a reference to https://www.youtube.com/@ChanceRoll-q2n!

### Fixes
- Originally considered an issue but now a feature, Monster Yam will remain vulnerable to the Putrid Scent effect
- Moved Hoss.png out of the item directory to prevent it from becoming part of the texture atlas
