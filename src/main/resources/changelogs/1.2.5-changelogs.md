## Forgotten Frights Patch || v1.2.5 Forge-1.20.1

### Notes
- We now have a wiki you can use to search any questions you may have regarding the mod! 
You can check it out at: https://dungeonsdelight.wiki.gg/

- This (hopefully), should be the final update for Dungeon's Delight 1.20.1 for the time being, I'm sure small translation 
and bug patches will pop up here and there but other than that I'm set on moving on to 1.21.1 for Dungeon's Delight.

### Important
- Stained Scrap Chains have been removed, all instances of Stained Scrap Chains will be replaced with normal Chains

### Additions
- A new category of food has been added known as "slime foods", these foods have a chance to not be consumed, below is a list of all slime foods:
  - Chloropasta | 10% chance
  - Ghoulash | 12% chance
  - Gelled Salad | 12% chance
  - Bowl of Silverfish and Chips | 20% chance
  - Bowl of Glow Berry Gelatin | 20% chance
  - Plate of Guardian Angel | 25% chance
- Pouncing now reduces fall damage by 75% (+5% per additional level) and in turn also reduces the minimum height you can take fall damage
- Pouncing no longer increases movement speed by +5% per level
- Added Stained Lanterns, not to be confused with Living Lanterns

### Food Rebalance Changes
- Spider Pie Slice now grants Pouncing II for 0:30 instead of Speed I for 0:30
- Shiokara now grants Regeneration II and Putrid Scent for 7 seconds
- Shiokara now requires a Ghast Tear
- Bloody Mary's Bleeding length has been decreased from 0:10 to 0:07
- Chicken Jockey Sandwich only requires x1 bread instead of x2 and requires Raw Chicken instead of Cooked Chicken
- Terrine Loaf now grants Exudation II instead of Exudation I
- Terrine Loaf now requires 1x Slime Noodles instead of 1x Slime Slab
- Poi's Exudation length has been increased from 2:00 to 5:00
- Monster Muffins can now be eaten fast
- Monster Muffins now require 1x Rotbulb instead of 2x Rotbulbs
- Spider Extract's recipe has been changed from 2x Spider Meat and 1x Nether Wart to 1x Spider Meat and 1x Spider Eye
- Poisonous Poutine now requires 1 less Poison Potatoes
- Raw Snifferwurst has been adjusted to grant 6 seconds of Regeneration at a 40% chance instead of 5 seconds at a 50% chance
- Cooked Snifferwurst has been adjusted to grant 8 seconds of Regeneration at a 60% chance instead of 100% chance
- Cooked Snifferwurst's saturation has been reduced from 0.9 to 0.7
- Ominous Omelette's Regeneration length has been increased from 0:10 to 0:12
- Snuffledog's Regeneration II length has been increased from 0:04 to 0:06
- Soft Served Sniffer Egg's nutrition has been decreased from 5 to 4
- Soft Served Sniffer Egg's Regeneration length has been decreased from 0:10 to 0:05
- Sliced foods now display their food effect tooltip (this is inconsistent with Farmer's Delight but whatever)
- Gelled Salad now grants 8 nutrition instead of 10 nutrition
- Gelled Salad no longer requires Sweet Berries
- Gelled Salad now requires 2x Slime Noodles instead of 1x Slime Slab
- Tokayaki now grants Exudation II for 2:00
- Amethyst Rock Candy now only grants Strength 70% of the time
- Bowl of Ossobuco now only refills one random monster effect instead of all
- Bowl of Ossobuco now takes 64 (3.2s) ticks to consume rather than 32 (1.6s) ticks
- Gyudon no longer requires Slime Noodles
- Silverfish and Chips no longer have the effects of a poison food
- Chloropasta no longer requires 1x Moss Block
- Chloropasta now takes 32 (1.6s) ticks to consume instead of 48 (2.4s) ticks
- Chloropasta's Regeneration II now only has a 50% chance to apply instead of 100% chance
- Devilish Eggs' nutrition has been increased from 4 to 6
